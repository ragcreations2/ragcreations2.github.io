About Us

A small studio with a big eye for detail

Sanjay Interiors was founded with one belief: good design should make everyday life easier and more joyful. We listen first, then design around how you actually live and work.

Our in-house team handles everything — concept, 3D visualisation, material selection, carpentry, electrical, painting and final styling — so you deal with one accountable partner from start to finish.

  • Transparent, itemised quotations
  • Dedicated project manager for every site
  • Branded materials with written warranties
  • On-time handover commitment
